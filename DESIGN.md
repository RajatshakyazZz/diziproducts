# VaultWorld Store — Design System (Premium Cyan Edition)

## Design Direction
A premium, futuristic, trustworthy digital commerce experience. It delivers the visual polish and precision of a venture-backed SaaS commerce product with a high-converting digital storefront.

- **Tone:** Modern, minimal, sophisticated, confident, and clean.
- **Visual Identity:** Fresh Cyan (`#06B6D4` / `#0891B2`) paired with deep navy contrast (`#0F172A` / `#164E63`), crisp white surfaces, and subtle cyan-tinted fills.
- **Strictly Removed:** All outdated purple promotional headers, orange/yellow CTA buttons, warm beige grid backgrounds, and festive/Diwali styling.

## Typography & Scale
- **Font Family:** `Inter`, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Hero Display:** 2.5rem – 3.75rem, font-black, tracking-tight
- **Section Headings:** 1.875rem – 2.25rem, font-extrabold, tracking-tight
- **Card Titles:** 1.125rem – 1.25rem, font-bold
- **Body Text:** 0.9375rem – 1rem, font-normal / font-medium, leading-relaxed
- **Metadata & Badges:** 0.75rem – 0.8125rem, font-semibold / font-bold, tracking-wide

---

## Radius & Elevation
- **Input Fields:** 12px – 14px (`rounded-xl`)
- **Buttons:** 12px – 14px (`rounded-xl`)
- **Cards & Banners:** 20px – 24px (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`)
- **Modals & Drawers:** 24px (`rounded-3xl`)
- **Shadows:**
  - Card: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`
  - Elevated: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`
  - Cyan Button: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`
  - Dock: `0 -10px 30px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
