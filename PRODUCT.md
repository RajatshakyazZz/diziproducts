# VaultWorld Store — Product Context

## Product Overview
VaultWorld Store (`vaultworld.shop`) is an ultra-fast, high-converting digital storefront built with Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. The store specializes in curated, ready-to-post vertical reels bundles (1080P, 9:16 MP4 format with audio, zero watermarks, and commercial/reseller rights) across 129+ niches (AI Reels, Story Reels, Comedy, Spiritual/God, Fitness, ASMR, etc.).

## Target Audience
- Instagram Theme Page Creators
- YouTube Shorts & Social Media Marketers
- Faceless Channel Operators
- Digital Product Resellers in India looking for high-margin digital goods

## Core Value Proposition
- Instant Google Drive delivery upon payment
- 100% Watermark-free, 1080P HD MP4 vertical video files
- Dual license option: Personal Use vs Reseller Rights
- Frictionless 1-step checkout supporting Indian UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, QR) via Cashfree
- Passwordless customer download portal (`/my-order`)
- Comprehensive self-serve reseller program (`/earn` & `/r/[slug]`)
